## GLOSSARY

A *Glossary* is a list of concepts, words or acronyms with brief explanations
of how they are used and understood in a context.


| *Term* | *Explanation*  |
| ---    | ---            |
| *Product Manager* or *Product Owner (PO)* | *Role* that is responsible for *"managing the product"* of defining product features (*requirements* and *acceptance criteria*) and their delivery time lines. |
| *Product Roadmap*       | Time-line (x-axis in months or quarters) showing when features are made available for a product.<br>Example: [*Java Product Roadmap*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Java_version_history) with releases over the years and also extending out into the future. |
| *Project Manager*       | *Role* that is responsible for *"managing the project"* in all aspecpts such as people (hiring), budget, setting goals (milestones) and delivering goals. |
| *Business Process (BP)* | Structured, repeatable, long-lasting (weeks, months, years) sequence of activities performed by people, systems, or organizations to achieve a specific business goal. Example: Customer-Registration process. |
| *Business Object (BO)*  | Data entity (typically a database record) that represents a collection of information that the system tracks and manages. Example: Customer-Registration record. |
| --- | --- |
